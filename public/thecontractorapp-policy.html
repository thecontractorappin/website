Privacy Policy for "The Contractor App" Effective Date: July 24, 2026 Company: RHMRAstute Solutions India Astute Solutions India ("we," "our," or "us") operates The Contractor App (the "App"), a B2B Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform for construction management, inventory tracking, and workforce administration. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application. 1. Information We Collect We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked to you or your company ("Personal Information"). Since this is an enterprise application, data is primarily managed by the subscribing Company ("Tenant") and their authorized administrators. A. Information You Provide to Us: Account & Profile Data: Full name, email address, mobile number, username, designation, and basic salary (for payroll modules). Business Information: Company name, PAN, GSTIN, and company codes. Media & Files: Photos (e.g., profile pictures, attendance selfies, machinery logs, expense bills) and PDF documents (e.g., Sub-Contractor bills, work orders) uploaded to your workspace. Financial Information: Transaction records, personal ledger entries, and expense diary logs. (Note: All subscription and marketplace payments are processed securely by our payment gateway; we do not store your credit card numbers on our servers). B. Information Collected Automatically: Location Data: We collect precise GPS coordinates (Latitude/Longitude) only when a user explicitly uses the Attendance module to "Clock In" or "Clock Out," or when logging heavy machinery start/stop meters. We do not track user location continuously in the background. Device Information: We collect Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to send you push notifications regarding tasks, approvals, and system alerts. 2. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve the App, specifically to: Facilitate account creation, user login, and secure authentication via OTPs and secure tokens. Manage daily construction operations, including labor attendance, machinery logs, and inventory dispatching. Process SaaS subscription billing and marketplace orders. Send administrative communications, such as push notifications for pending approvals, task assignments, and password resets. Generate automated financial ledgers, salary payslips, and project expenditure reports. 3. Sharing of Your Information We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data with third-party service providers that strictly facilitate the core functionality of our platform: Cloud Infrastructure: Google Cloud / Firebase (for secure photo and document storage) and MongoDB (for database hosting). Communication APIs: Twilio (for SMS verification and OTPs) and Nodemailer (for system emails). Payment Gateways: Razorpay (to securely process SaaS subscriptions and marketplace transactions). 4. Data Security We implement strict, industry-standard security measures to protect your data. User passwords are mathematically hashed before being stored. All data transmissions between the App and our servers are encrypted using Secure Socket Layer (SSL/HTTPS) technology. However, please be aware that no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure. 5. Data Retention & Deletion We retain your data for as long as your company maintains an active or paused subscription with us. Account Deletion: Staff Users: If you are a staff member, your data is controlled by your Company Administrator. Please contact them to delete your account or correct your data. Company Admins: You can request complete termination of your workspace and deletion of your tenant database by contacting our support team. Upon a verified request, we will purge your specific database container and all associated files from our active servers. 6. User Permissions Requested by the App To function correctly, The Contractor App requests the following device permissions: Camera & Photo Library: Required for uploading expense bills, capturing attendance selfies, and documenting machinery conditions. Location Services: Required strictly for geofencing and validating clock-in/clock-out coordinates for payroll and machinery tracking accuracy. Push Notifications: Required to alert users of pending task approvals, new tenders, and inventory updates. 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy at any time. We will alert you about any changes by updating the "Effective Date" of this Privacy Policy and sending a platform broadcast or push notification for significant updates. 8.
